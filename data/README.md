# Published research data

- `higgs/`: pinned source/projection hashes, deterministic cohort/site assignment and TRAIN question banks. Source HIGGS data is hosted at https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/280/higgs (CC BY 4.0). Full 11-million-record arrays and incomplete download files are not mirrored in Git. Use `scripts/prepare_higgs_source.py` and `scripts/prepare_higgs_workload.py` to reconstruct the projection, checking the source SHA-256 first. The reserved original FINAL bank is not released or scored.
- `../outputs/public_mac_calibration/`: 360 TRAIN and 216 SELECT records, retaining all 12 failed trials; relative durations, plaintext frame costs and reference/transport validity.
- `../outputs/public_ec2/`: all 168 session records and packet metadata at both observation points for seven modes. IP lengths include transport headers and ACKs; payload and addresses are removed. All packets, including setup/inter-session cover/teardown, are retained. Oracle session labels are accounting annotations, never attacker features. T2 and T3 observe the same multiplexed segment, not separate logical sites.
- `../outputs/public_model_checks/`: 48 repaired typed-plan/structured-summary checks; no model weights.
- `../outputs/public_local_tls/`: initial (one retained receive failure), repaired and final-frequency phases, with all 294 acquired TLS sessions and packet metadata. Deliberate failures remain failures. Clock origins are shifted per phase. Three logical loopback endpoints are one physical host.
- Other dated `outputs/` directories contain derived tables, features and explicitly labelled synthetic mechanism/workflow studies. `outputs/synthetic_demo/` is a software smoke example, not an acquired experimental result.

`DEIDENTIFICATION.json` links original capture hashes to the publication derivatives. `DATA_SHA256.json` covers every published data payload. No password, SSH key, TLS private key, OAuth/recovery credential, live public endpoint or raw packet payload is part of the public data. Original unredacted operational archives are not included. The small sample and exploratory intervals do not certify a completion probability of 0.999, wire privacy or measured energy savings.
